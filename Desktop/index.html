For what purpose is this used?

This app helps users easily calculate their electricity bill based on units consumed and current tariff rates. It provides a simple way to track power usage, estimate monthly costs, and pay electricity bills online securely. Users can view past bills, receive payment reminders, and avoid late fees. The app is designed to be user-friendly and suitable for all users, making electricity bill management fast and hassle-free
